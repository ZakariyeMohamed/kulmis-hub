About KULMIS HUB

KULMIS HUB waa madal dijitaal ah oo diiradda saarta baahinta fursadaha waxbarasho, seminars, shaqooyin iyo fursado kale.

Waxaan isku dayeynaa inaan hal meel ku soo ururino fursadaha gudaha iyo kuwa caalamiga ah.

Waxaan baahinnaa:

  • 🎓 Scholarships & Educational Opportunities
  • 🎤 Gudaha & Caalami Seminars
  • 💼 Gudaha & Caalami Work Opportunities
  • 🌍 Caalami Opportunities

Aragtideenna

In KULMIS HUB noqoto meel lagu kalsoon yahay oo laga helo xog iyo fursado waxbarasho, shaqo iyo horumarineed.

Hadafkeenna

Inaan bulshada siino xog faa'iido leh, fudud oo la fahmi karo, isla markaana aan dadka ku hagno meelaha rasmiga ah.
